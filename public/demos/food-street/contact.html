FAQ

Common Questions

What are the opening hours? +
We are open every day from 10:00 AM to 12:00 AM (midnight). For the best experience with all vendors and live events, visit us between 6PM and 10PM.
Is there an entry fee? +
No! Entry to Old Monk Food Street is completely free. You only pay for what you eat and drink. Even our live events are free to attend.
Is it family-friendly? +
Absolutely! We have a dedicated kids' play area, table service, clean facilities and a variety of food options for all ages. Families are our most valued guests.
Can I book the stage for a private event? +
Yes! Our riverside stage is available for private events, performances and promotions. Contact us via Instagram DM at @old_monk_food_street or use the form above.
Is parking available? +
Yes, free parking is available for both cars and two-wheelers. The parking area is right outside the food street entrance on River View Road.
Are there vegetarian options? +
Definitely! Almost all our vendors offer vegetarian options — from momos and dosas to chaat, juices and desserts. Vegan options are also available on request.
