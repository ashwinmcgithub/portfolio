Our Story

Where Every
Bite Feels
Like Home

Nestled along the banks of the Thodupuzha River, Old Monk Food Street is more than a food court — it's a gathering place for food lovers, families, and friends. Seven curated vendors, a stage for live events, and 60+ seats with river views await you.

  • 7 handpicked culinary vendors under one roof
  • Live events on our riverside stage
  • Kids' play area for family outings
  • Open daily 10 AM – 12 AM
  • Located on River View Road, Thodupuzha
  • Free parking available for visitors
