Panchakarma Therapy

Complete body detoxification through 5 purification procedures. Removes deep-rooted toxins and rejuvenates the body.

Duration: 7-21 days

Abhyangam (Oil Massage)

Full body warm oil massage using medicated herbal oils. Relieves stress, improves circulation, and nourishes the skin.

Duration: 45-60 mins
💧

Shirodhara

Continuous stream of warm medicated oil on the forehead. Excellent for insomnia, anxiety, migraines, and mental clarity.

Duration: 30-45 mins
🧂

Njavarakizhi

Bolus therapy with medicated rice. Strengthens muscles, rejuvenates skin, and is highly effective for neurological disorders.

Duration: 45 mins
🌱

Elakizhi (Leaf Bundle)

Herbal leaf poultice massage. Excellent for joint pain, arthritis, spondylitis, and sports injuries.

Duration: 45 mins
⚡

Kativasti

Warm medicated oil pooled on the lower back. Highly effective for lower back pain, sciatica, and lumbar spondylosis.

Duration: 30 mins
